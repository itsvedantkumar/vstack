## Corrected statements for MISREAD entries

- **Entry 10 (claude-code-bench).** The confidence note's "a single model" is wrong. Per the fetched REPORT.md ranking table, the nine experiments used three models: Opus (runs #1, #2, #3, #5), Sonnet (#4, #6, #7), and Haiku (#8, #9), and Finding 1 ("Prompt engineering > model selection") is explicitly a model-contrast result. The fragility critique is still supportable — n=9 runs of one task, self-chosen composite weights — but the accurate statement is "a single agent CLI (Claude Code) with three Claude-family models", not "a single model".
- **Entry 17 (arXiv:2607.27250).** The report overstates the paper's hedge. The abstract says borderline task difficulty being agent-specific (Spearman rho=0.75) is "offering **a candidate explanation** for prior contradictions", not "the explanation". The corrected statement: the paper proposes agent-specific borderline difficulty as *a candidate* explanation for prior contradictory results (single-agent studies drawing tasks from different agents' informative bands).

## Corrections for MISREAD entries

- **Entry 1**: The context-engineering study is **Section 5** ("Results: Context Engineering") of the HTML full text, not section 6. Section 6 is "Results: Monitoring" (LLM-monitor detection rates of 86–89% on Impossible-LiveCodeBench, 42–65% on Impossible-SWEbench).
- **Entry 2**: The statement that fine-tuning not to be sycophantic reduces successful reward tampering by up to a factor of 10 is in **Section 5.3** ("Training away sycophancy does not eliminate reward-tampering"), alongside Figure 8 — not section 3. The quote and Figure 8 reference themselves are correct.
- **Entry 4**: METR's blog states o3 "reward-hacks in **0.7%** of runs across all HCAST tasks", not 1.7%. (RE-Bench is separately reported at 39/128 = 30.4%, with >43× higher hacking on RE-Bench than HCAST; the 0.7% figure is the HCAST-wide number the report attributes.)
- **Entry 10**: In the Claude 4.1 System Card, "Reward hacking" is **Section 5**, and Table 5.B appears on **printed pages 15–17** (the table straddles pages 16–17), not section 6 / page 75. All numeric values and quotes attributed to Table 5.B are otherwise correct as stated. (In the companion Claude 4 System Card the section is numbered 6 and the table is 6.2.A, on ~page 76–77 — the report's locator matches neither card for Table 5.B.)
